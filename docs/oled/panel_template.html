Notes

The look is the HDD Synth's: a 128 × 64 SSD1306, a lit title bar, the same 5 × 7 font, and the same encoder and button handling, copied from HDDSynth_Native so the two products feel like one family.

That font has no descenders, so screen text avoids lower-case g, j, p, q and y where it matters ("New disk", not "Changing disk").

The head trace is the one screen only this drive can show: it knows exactly where its head is, so seeks draw themselves.

On the board, a full frame over I2C takes about 25 ms of core 0, so frames will be paced (the HDD Synth draws at most 10 a second) or sent by DMA, to keep the audio and the cylinder loads on time.
